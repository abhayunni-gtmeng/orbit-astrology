How the reading is derived

Zodiac: normalize longitude to 0–360°, divide by 30 and round down. Index 0 is Aries, 1 Taurus, continuing through index 11 Pisces. The remainder is degrees within the sign.

Aspects: shortest angular distance = min(|A − B|, 360 − |A − B|). Compare with 0°, 60°, 90°, 120° and 180°. Orbit includes a contact if its distance from an exact angle is no more than 3°.

Whole-sign houses: house = ((planet sign index − rising sign index + 12) mod 12) + 1. Birth-time charts calculate the ascendant from the eastern horizon’s intersection with the ecliptic. This page shows weekly planetary data, not a replay of your birth chart.

Interpretation: the resulting signs, houses and aspects select editorial themes, actions and questions. These suggestions are not scientific conclusions or guaranteed events.
